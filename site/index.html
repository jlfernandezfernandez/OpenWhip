OpenWhip

A tiny menu-bar whip for when your coding agent needs a little encouragement.

Crack it, it types FASTER into whatever has focus — terminal, Teams, Slack, your IDE chat — and presses Enter.

Download GitHub

Grab it, swing the mouse hard, listen for the crack. Click to drop.
